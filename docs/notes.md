# Terminology

Transaction: any movement of money
├─ Income: money added to an account
├─ Expense: money leaving
├─ Transfer: movement of money between accounts
└─ Withdrawal: cash taken out of account

---

Expense: type
├─ Purchase
├─ Payment
├─ Subscription
└─ Fee

Expense: frequency
├─ one-time
└─ recurring

Expense: amount behavior
├─ fixed
└─ variable

--- or ---

Expense Type
├─ Purchase
├─ Payment
└─ Fee

Expense Attributes
├─ Subscription
├─ Recurring
├─ Fixed
└─ Variable

Purchase: Money spent voluntarily to acquire goods or services.
Payment: Money spent to satisfy a financial obligation, bill, debt, or required commitment.
Subscription: Money spent for recurring access to a product or service.
Fee: Money charged as a cost, penalty, or service charge.
